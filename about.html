About Me

I am a Vietnamese American who loves character design and vast world building. I graduated from Louisiana State University with a bachelors in Digital Art. In the fall of 2025, I will be pursuing my masters in Digital Media and Engineering to gain more expereince in video game making and film.

I use Clip Studio Paint, Procreate, Zbrush, Maya, After Effects, and Photoshop in my work. I'm currently familiarizing myself with Unreal Engine and have made 2D assets for games made with this program.
